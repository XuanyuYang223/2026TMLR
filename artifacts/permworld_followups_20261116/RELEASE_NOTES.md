# PermWorld follow-ups: saved results and writing evidence

Six completed exploratory result bundles, with four new units (unit02–unit05) and the historical unit reported separately. This is a publication of existing outputs, not another experiment or a confirmation claim.

Read the [result index](https://github.com/XuanyuYang223/2026TMLR/blob/main/artifacts/permworld_followups_20261116/README.md), [closing scientific report](https://github.com/XuanyuYang223/2026TMLR/blob/main/diagnostics/permworld_closing_audit_20261110/README.md), and [claim–evidence–scope table](https://github.com/XuanyuYang223/2026TMLR/blob/main/diagnostics/permworld_closing_audit_20261110/writing/claim_evidence_scope.md).

Attachments contain actual content-addressed bytes for reports/code, large tables, fitted probes/scalers/maps, per-example readout and composition scores, intervention outputs and gradient vectors. They preserve the original result bytes and include safe restoration mappings for files formerly reached through absolute local symlinks.

The [manifest and restore instructions](https://github.com/XuanyuYang223/2026TMLR/tree/main/artifacts/permworld_followups_20261116) bind asset/object SHA-256. Raw intermediate recurrent/token-state caches (42,733,696,512 bytes) and preexisting base checkpoints/input activation dependencies are explicitly excluded, retained locally, and are not claimed as published here.

Scientific boundaries remain unchanged: task-related transferable readout does not establish shared computation; the registered one-dimensional edit did not change selected counts; gradients are nonzero; the predicted E/D encoding crossover failed; explicit composition did not beat the same-specialist-twice baseline. Existing manuscripts, F17/Cosyne material and older releases were not replaced.
