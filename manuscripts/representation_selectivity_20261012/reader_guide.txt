Working paper: When Do Mathematical Correspondences Show Up in Representational Similarity? A Controlled Study

Read manuscript.pdf first. The English manuscript uses the official TMLR preprint template. This is a working draft, not a submitted or published article.

Structure
1 Introduction: research question and three contributions.
2 Related work: task overlap/RSA task taxonomy, representation convergence, metric ground truth/deconfounding/permutation calibration, and permutation encodings.
3 Design: explain what the two comparisons answer before defining B0/Bt/dc/du/Delta, fixed-T task candidates, and shared initialization; Figure 1 is the schematic and Table 1 gives coverage.
4 Results: whether correspondences show selectivity; which parts change with training/encoding; scope across layers, metrics, and mathematical systems.
5 Discussion: four reporting recommendations for researchers using similarity to argue for shared structure.
6 Limitations: first, the lack of independent-initialization replication in PermWorld; then capacity/proficiency, answer-stratum support, dependence/sampling, and measurement/mechanism.
7 Conclusion and full appendices.

Important qualifications
- 29/32 above the other-task mean and 19/32 uniquely first refer to answer-stratified, final-norm ordinary CKA. Raw counts are 30/32 and 25/32.
- The 15 candidates include 1 designated task; the non-designated mean has denominator 14. The 1/15 chance reference assumes exchangeability and no ties. No binomial test treats the 32 dependent conditions as independent samples.
- Shared initial weights make the raw fixed-T initial task contrast 0. Candidate-specific groups/sample support mean that answer-stratified contrasts need not be 0; the formulas and text include this qualification.
- GRU exceedances/deficiencies attain 99–100% in both encodings and trained Bt remains positive. Changing B0 reverses Delta; this is not a reversal of trained relationship preference.
- Graph reuses Perm's actual Transformer initialization and is not an independent-initialization replication. Both data and initialization change across the two Matrix units, precluding attribution purely to initialization.
- R1 is one relationship in two units, used illustratively. Size-equal controlled CKA is negative while kNN/Procrustes are positive; no claim says every n agrees. R1 F1/F2 measured accuracy is 100%, which is not generalized to all matrix tasks.
- R1's designated fixed-T answer-controlled candidate also ranks first at initialization; its candidate advantage decreases with training. This is a different question from the correct/wrong input comparison.
- Matrix's original 64 passed / 2 failed / 6 undefined_consistent and scientific_analysis_complete=false remain; the two failed output-probability kNN values are not modified.
