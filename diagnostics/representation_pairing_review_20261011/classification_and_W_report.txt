Mathematical catalogue and saved-input wrong-transform audit

This catalogue was fixed before the new representation-pairing CKA calculation. It contains 120 PermWorld mathematical cells (8 relations x 15 right tasks excluding the left task) and 12 GraphWorld cells (4 x 3). There are 12 designated rows, no additionally proved other_known_exact rows, and 120 not_designated rows. The empty category is retained. Not_designated means not included in this known-exact binary catalogue; it does not mean mathematically independent or unrelated. No finite candidate search was used to prove absence of a relation, and no new CKA was used for classification.

Proofs and actual registry references are in classification_audit.json. formula is an asserted exact identity only for designated rows. comparison_formula for other rows is the inherited original mapping applied to a candidate right task; it is a proposed comparison equation, not a universal mathematical assertion. The original offset is preserved for every candidate within a relation, including components(C(pi))-1 for decomposition_complement.

All saved input anchors were retained in W_actual_per_anchor.csv: 10752 distinct PermWorld test inputs (512 at each n=10,...,30), and 1536 distinct GraphWorld analysis inputs (512 at n=6,7,8). The 92160 relation-anchor rows reuse those base inputs. Architecture and encoding do not create extra inputs or independent worlds. All 12 original correct formulas hold on all their saved anchors. No model or metric was run, no new input generated, and no original transform changed.

The 372 per-length/control retained counts reproduce the original completed CKA tables exactly: 336 PermWorld raw/answer-strata cells and 36 GraphWorld raw/answer/density-strata cells. W_reader_validation.json independently reaggregates all booleans and 28 pooled/equal-length summaries. Graph labels were also checked directly against the already saved five-action adjacency arrays.

Original selection rules:
PermWorld: joint [n,left(identity),right(identity),right(complement),right(inverse)] after the original right-label offset. Retain strata with count >=3 independently in validation and test. The joint keys include correct and wrong answers, not only the correctly transformed answer.
GraphWorld: joint [n,left(identity),mapped-right(identity),mapped-right(correct),mapped-right(wrong)]. Density adds the identity-input edge count. Retain strata with count >=3 independently in validation and analysis. This audit keeps all raw anchors and adds retention flags; it never deletes coincidentally formula-preserving wrong-transform cases.
