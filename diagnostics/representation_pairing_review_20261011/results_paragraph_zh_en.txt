中文
在答案分层、最终层及逐长度等权的主分析中，PermWorld的32个条件有26个表现出正的配对选择性。选择性随编码、层和控制改变；相对于匹配初始化的训练增量需另行解释。在新增的固定输入变换、改变右任务对照中，答案分层后指定对应超过未列入对应候选均值的条件为PermWorld29/32、GraphWorld3/4；这些条件共享模型和输入，不是独立重复。这一新对照不等同于原Bt/Delta，也不识别任务关系的纯因果效应。正确数学对应常伴随几何选择性，但当前证据不单独识别数学结构机制或功能收益。

English
In the answer-stratified, final-layer analysis with equal weighting across lengths, 26 of 32 PermWorld conditions exhibit positive pairing selectivity. Selectivity varies with encoding, layer and control, while training increments relative to matched saved initializations require separate interpretation. In the new fixed-transform, varying-right-task control, designated correspondences exceed the mean of candidates not designated for that correspondence in 29/32 PermWorld and 3/4 GraphWorld answer-stratified conditions. These conditions share models and inputs and are not independent replications. The new score contrasts are distinct from the original Bt and Delta and do not identify a pure causal effect of the mathematical relation. Exact mathematical correspondences often accompany geometric selectivity, but these exploratory observations alone do not identify a mathematical-structure mechanism or functional benefit.
