The first benchmark failed the unchanged2e-5 tolerance on an independent
prefix-only smaller-batch intermediate-layer path. The repair uses the exact
same full-prompt128-example shape for manual-versus-hook validation. It retains
the original2e-5 gate, extraction code, metrics and fixed endpoint. Prefix-only
raw-layer deviations remain explicitly reported as numerical sensitivity;
prefix-only final normalization and new-versus-old final arrays also pass.
The contrast parser was aligned to the actual column names nan_reason and
initial_similarity/trained_similarity/similarity_change before full metrics.
This repair did not alter the formulas or original values.

Output and learning controls

Initial answer CE was not originally cached: all2016 cells remain explicitly
missing. Initial answer accuracy remains available from original predictions.

separately; EOS is not a measure of mathematical-task learning.

for validation/test. Main geometry is jointly answer-stratified; confidence and
prediction-error structure are not thereby removed.
