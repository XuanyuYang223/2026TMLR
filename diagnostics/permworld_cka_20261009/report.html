Final four questions

  1. Which relationships stably exceed the initialization reference?Both inversion relationships, descents--inverse descents and exceedances--deficiencies, have consistently positive Delta under answer and input-kernel sensitivities. This exceeds a program-reconstructed reference only; original initial-weight identity remains directly unverifiable.
  2. Does the result depend on task pair, length, or metric?Neither inversion relationship depends on a single length/seed/one of the three metrics, but the overall conclusion depends strongly on relationship family and answer control. The six complement cases lack equivalent advantages, and a macro mean does not imply a universal effect.
  3. Do more tasks accompany greater initialization retention?Across five splits, k16 all exceed k1; three full trajectories are monotone, while R1/R3 decrease at intermediate steps. Original shared initial weights remain unverified, and input/model pairs are dependent.
  4. Which open question can an encoding control address next?It can test whether the inversion-contrast increment depends on current numeric token, positions, and one-line sequence encoding or persists under another encoding preserving the exact mathematical relationships. Each encoding still needs its own initialization and answer/output references; CKA alone cannot establish an internal algebraic mechanism. That experiment is not run in this round.
