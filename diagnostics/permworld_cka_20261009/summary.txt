2. Does the conclusion depend on a task pair, length, or metric?
Each inversion relationship remains positive under leave-one-length and three-metric checks; the overall claim depends on relationship type and answer control. Across eight relationships, ordinary-CKA macro mean Delta is +0.251 and median −0.022; after answer control, mean −0.109 and median −0.290, with a negative macro mean after leaving out any one relationship. Positive Delta denotes a change in the correct/wrong relative contrast, not an absolute increase in correct-pair CKA. CKA cannot identify an encoding or mathematical mechanism.

A/B reconstructed parameters are identical, but original shared initial weights are not directly verified; these two CKA measurements cannot exactly decompose the sources of similarity.

It can test whether the inversion-contrast increment depends on the present numeric tokens, positions, and one-line sequence encoding, or persists under another encoding that preserves the mathematical relationships. Soft-output and general input effects have not been fully ruled out. No encoding experiment was conducted in this round.
