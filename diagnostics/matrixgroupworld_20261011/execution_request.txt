B. Exact mathematical objects and tasks
Elements of F2 are 0/1; matrix multiplication and vector addition are modulo 2. wt counts the ones in a binary vector using ordinary integer addition, not modulo 2.
Let e1 and e2 be standard basis vectors and s=e1+e2. The four properties are:
  F1(A)=wt(A e1): weight of the first column.
  F2(A)=wt(A^T e1): weight of the first row.
  F3(A)=wt(A s): weight after elementwise XOR of the first two columns.
  F4(A)=wt(A^T s): weight after elementwise XOR of the first two rows.
Each model predicts one property only. Answers lie in 1..n and have the same theoretical label distribution under uniform GL sampling:
  Pr(Fj=k)=C(n,k)/(2^n-1), k=1..n.
Fix the final mode baseline from actual training labels. The theoretical distribution is only a mathematical audit and does not replace actual data.

Let Q=I+E21, where E21 has a one in row 2, column 1; Q^2=I and Q s=e1.
P swaps the first two coordinates. Apply all transformations to actual matrices and then use the common encoder.

The four frozen directed relations (left f and right g satisfy g(T(A))=f(A)) are:
  R1 [primary]: left F1, right F2; correct T=A^T; wrong U=P A^T.
  R2 [supplementary]: left F3, right F4; correct T=A^T; wrong U=Q^T A^T.
  R3 [primary]: left F1, right F3; correct T=A Q; wrong U=A Q^T.
  R4 [supplementary]: left F2, right F4; correct T=Q^T A; wrong U=Q A.
The mathematical audit must also verify:
  R1 wrong answer = weight of the second column;
  R2 wrong answer = weight of the first column;
  R3 wrong answer = weight of the second column;
  R4 wrong answer = weight of the second row.
Wrong answers may accidentally equal the left answer. Record the actual rate and retain these inputs.

Transposition is a group antiautomorphism; left/right multiplication by fixed group elements is a group action and must not be called a group homomorphism.
Relation instances are not independent relation types: this study covers only transposition and fixed multiplication actions.
These formulas also hold for general binary square matrices, and the weight tasks are related to graph degree statistics. The added structure is mainly finite-field linear mixing; using GL inputs alone does not establish a group-specific effect, learned group multiplication, or coverage of group theory.
