3. Conditional interpretation of S/Bt/B0/dc/dw/Delta

- Bt>0: under this metric/preprocessing/fixed-length aggregate, trained correct pairings are more similar than original wrong pairings. This does not automatically imply discovery of a mathematical algorithm.
- Delta>0: correct−wrong relative preference increases from genuine initialization. Read dc,dw first: correct can rise, wrong can fall, or both. If falling wrong similarity dominates, say “reshaped pairing selectivity,” not “substantial convergence of correct representations.”
- Delta<0 with Bt>0: trained preference may still favor correct pairings, but initialization had a larger preference. Do not say no relation was learned.
- Encoding reverses Delta while Bt does not reverse/is similar: interpret initial starting points and Γ0; do not claim reversed learned relation preference. Actual Γt sign/size changes support descriptions of encoding-dependent trained geometric preference.
- Hidden/output shared trends: report in parallel, without a supposed net mechanism from hiddenDelta−outputDelta. Answer stratification removes group means, not all nonlinear answer/confidence/prediction-error information.
- Retain full correct and identity score magnitudes; identity is not a prespecified upper bound or baseline that must be exceeded.
- Undefined S/Δ at a layer/metric/length marks variance/sample/rank/mathematical-definition boundaries, not no learning or no correspondence.
- Describe every relation’s architecture/encoding conditions before discussing patterns; eight instances cover only two transformation types and are not independent statistical repetitions.
