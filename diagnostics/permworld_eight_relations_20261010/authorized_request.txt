C. Stage 2: one small graph-theory pilot

C1. Objects, tasks, and relations

Objects: simple directed graphs without self-loops; A_ij=1 denotes i→j, and diagonal entries always remain 0.
Fix vertex counts to n=6,7,8. Compute each size separately, then aggregate with equal weight 1/3; do not confuse this with PermWorld’s 1/21.
Only four tasks:
- maximum out-degree;
- minimum out-degree;
- maximum in-degree;
- minimum in-degree.

Transformation R: reverse graph, transposing the adjacency matrix.
Transformation C: graph complement, applying 1-A_ij only off the diagonal.
Four relation instances:
- max_out_degree(G) = max_in_degree(RG);
- min_out_degree(G) = min_in_degree(RG);
- max_out_degree(G) = n-1-min_out_degree(CG);
- max_in_degree(G) = n-1-min_in_degree(CG).

The formulas follow directly from degree, reversal, and complementation definitions. They share four tasks and two transformations and are not four independent validations.
Labels are 0..n-1 and can directly use the original numeric tokens without introducing multi-token answers.
