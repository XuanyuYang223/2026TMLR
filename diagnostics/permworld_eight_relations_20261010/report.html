How mathematical correspondences change representational similarity: a controlled eight-relation PermWorld extension

This report includes all 64 single-task 20k endpoints from one existing data world, with one genuine initialization per architecture. Sixteen earlier endpoints and the verified results for two relations are reused, with at most 48 missing models added. No subset was selected by geometric direction, source-task accuracy, or undefined metrics. The eight relation instances cover only inversion and complement; the 32 conditions are not independent repetitions.

The order is original similarity S → trained correct/wrong pairing preference Bt → change relative to genuine initialization Δ. A reversal of Δ does not guarantee a reversal of Bt, and positive Δ does not guarantee increased correct-pair similarity. This is an exploratory extension without confirmatory p-values, equivalence claims, or mathematical-mechanism inference. Source-model capacities and task mastery are unmatched, so configuration comparisons do not identify a pure architecture effect.
