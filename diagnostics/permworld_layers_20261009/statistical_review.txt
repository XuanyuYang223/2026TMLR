Independent statistical verification of PermWorld multilayer results (exploratory; existing checkpoints only)

Scope and verification method
This review reads only layer_metric_results.csv and its summaries; the independent implementation is engineering/statistical_review.py, without importing summarize.py calculation functions. Python statistics.fmean/median/stdev recomputes each source using all 21 lengths 10–30, with weights of 1/21 each. If even one length is undefined, the source mean is undefined. Stratified analysis was not replaced by pooled or centered aggregate computation. Per-length tables, provenance, hashes, and all-branch numbers are in statistical_review.json.

Result: passed.
Raw cells: 187,488; source summaries: 8,928; relation summaries: 3,312; per-length summaries: 69,552; leave-one-relation/family summaries: 6,336. Every source has a complete length set. The maximum absolute difference in recomputed 21-length source means and relation statistics is 2.27e-13; the maximum difference in per-length source statistics is 1.82e-12. These differences arise from floating-point rounding across calculation paths. Check counts are scalar consistency checks, not experimental replicates or a measure of statistical evidence.

The 52,080 cells in the historical permutation_metric_results.csv were also compared field by field, mapping only the historical prefix_final_norm name to final_norm_end: every historical field is textually identical. Thus the new layer boundaries did not change original scores by recomputing the historical primary position.

All three fixed primary metrics linear_cka, knn_overlap, procrustes, and the debiased_cka sensitivity are retained; five layer positions each have end and value_mean, totaling ten contextual branches, plus four input/embedding branches and four answer/output/query branches. Both controls, all cohorts, and all available sources are output. The 24 all-branch/five-component heatmaps and 6 complete-length curves exist. No best-effect layer was selected.

Leave-one-relation and shared-family deletion is grouped strictly by cohort, kind, branch, control, and metric. Each relation first aggregates analyzable sources, then remaining relations receive equal weights; undefined relations do not cause remaining relations to be reweighted. Self-invariant and cross-task relations remain separate. independent_relation_inference is False throughout; source standard deviations describe initialization variation within the shared world, not confidence intervals for independent worlds. No new input-sampling uncertainty was generated for the current fixed inputs.

Preservation of undefined values
embedding_end and unordered embedding_value_mean are mathematically constant within a fixed length and have undefined metrics. In total, 2,629 source Delta values are undefined; this includes mathematical constants/answer controls and cells covering all lengths but unable to aggregate because individual lengths are insufficient. Under answer control, six_hour double_complement/double_reverse have only 19/21 defined kNN lengths and 20/21 debiased-CKA lengths, strictly retained as undefined. Individual output-probability Procrustes or answer-control cells also have undefined lengths and cannot be assumed to be zero effects. See the complete list in JSON.

Depth and initialization must be interpreted jointly
From block1 end to block2 end for the descents relation, Delta increases by approximately +0.4473, whereas Bt decreases by approximately 0.0108 and B0 becomes approximately 0.4581 more negative. Thus this between-layer change cannot be directly described as “the second layer first learned a stronger relation preference”; most of the between-layer Delta difference comes from initialization-reference geometry. Exceedance block1 to block2 shows both increasing Bt and declining B0. Every layer requires B0, Bt, dc, dw, and Delta side by side.

This can coexist with local positive Delta for the two inverse relations; one relation must not be generalized into an overall learning increment.

Answer, output, and input references
Answer stratification weakens, but does not eliminate, the two inverse relations' positive end Delta; within_length final-end ordinary-CKA Delta is approximately +0.993578/+1.379408, versus answer-stratified +0.588965/+0.641461. Output probabilities also have positive answer-stratified Delta +0.336123/+0.331151, which remains an interpretive limitation. Hidden Delta minus output Delta cannot estimate an additional mechanism.
Answer-stratified Delta for the positional numeric-embedding input reference is -0.120777/-0.082482, not reproducing these two positive hidden-state directions; it is a static input readout of positional token embeddings and also includes trained embedding-weight changes, so it cannot rule out more general encoding contributions. One-hot input references have no model-initialization condition; no Delta is invented for them. Constant fixed-length embeddings are not counterevidence and must not be converted into metrics using floating-point summation noise.

Interpretive scope and next steps
The current all-layer results continue to support “training changed relative transform-pairing preferences compared with constructor initialization, with changes depending on relation type, layer, and readout.” The two inverse relations have consistently positive Delta across fixed metrics from block2 to final and for mean pooling from block1; this is not substantial convergence of correct representations in every layer. All historical source initialization identities remain C: consistency is established only relative to references reconstructed with the original procedure, and original genuine step-0 identity remains unverified. This review does not distinguish encoding/output-related geometry from independent algebraic mechanisms.

The authorized next encoding × architecture development pilot should report Gamma0, Gammat, Gamma(dc), Gamma(dw), and GammaDelta: with genuine shared step-0 and underlying inputs, examine whether encoding changes mainly concern initialization B0, trained Bt, or individual correct/wrong changes. Development worlds and models are excluded from formal confirmation; learnability and engineering-restoration consistency do not pass based on Gamma signs. This statistical review does not start training, GroupWorld, null-space, or larger-model experiments.
