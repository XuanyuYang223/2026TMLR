Interpretive points
1. Encoding clearly changes the measured relative initialization preference in this development world: in both architectures, one-line
   inverse B0 is negative and complement B0 positive; cycle notation reverses these directions. Initialization directions agree across all 21 lengths, three
   primary metrics, and debiased readouts. This review adds no statistical test.
2. Both architectures have negative inverse GammaDelta across all 21 lengths, three primary metrics, and debiased CKA;
   peaks/valleys are positive throughout. This supports different pairing-selectivity reshaping under the prespecified encoding intervention, but
   GammaDelta alone does not establish a trained-preference reversal. In particular, GRU inverse CKA Bt is positive for both encodings
   (+.067502, +.079276), with Gammat only +.011774; GammaDelta=-1.406106
   largely reflects Gamma0=+1.417880. Its per-length Gammat is positive for CKA at only 10/21 lengths,
   and Procrustes at 16/21; trained encoding differences cannot be assumed to share one direction.
3. Transformer inverse answer-stratified Bt does change from positive one-line (+.093118) to negative cycle
   (-.032044), consistently across three primary metrics; however, source-task mastery is clearly unmatched,
   and there is only one unit, so this does not confirm a universal trained-preference reversal or encoding mechanism.
   Both architectures and encodings have positive peaks/valleys Bt, but cycle Bt is lower than one-line;
   positive peaks/valleys GammaDelta cannot mean that cycle training produces stronger correct-relation preference.
4. Positive Delta for one-line inverse mainly reflects wrong-pair declines: Transformer dc=+.053146,
   dw=-.644855, GRU dc=+.024350, dw=-.660075; this is not substantial convergence
   of correct representations. Negative cycle inverse Delta mainly reflects declining initially high correct CKA,
   and cannot alone establish no learning. In contrast, GRU one-line peaks/valleys improve on the correct side
   and decline on the wrong side, a different decomposition.
5. Individual conditions' Delta still depends on metric/control: GRU one-line peaks/valleys answer-controlled CKA
   is +.257354, Procrustes +.411554, neighborhood overlap -.008532 (positive at 11/21 lengths);
   Transformer one-line peaks/valleys ordinary-CKA raw Delta is +.023020 and answer-controlled
   Delta -.262120. Agreement in encoding GammaDelta direction across both controls/metrics does not mean each condition's
   training Delta direction is robust. Ordinary and debiased CKA agree in primary aggregate direction.
6. Output-related explanations remain open. Answer-controlled output_prob inverse CKA Delta for one-line
   is +.373154(T)/+.427486(G), versus -.684445/-.643132 for cycle; peaks/valleys respectively
   give -.302842/-.301157 and +.932516/+1.232557. Output probabilities also show large encoding
   interactions; hidden Delta minus output Delta cannot estimate an additional mechanism. output_prob is normalized only over
   numeric answers 0..30, not the full-vocabulary distribution.

But cycle notation gives approximately 30%..41% on new tests across four tasks; Transformer one-line approximately 73%..99%;
GRU one-line descents/peaks/valleys nearly 100%, with recoils only 39.47%. Engineering success establishes trainable task outputs
and a complete pipeline, not sufficiently learned source models or matched cross-encoding capability. Encoding
can affect initialization geometry and learning difficulty; current results cannot separate these two paths.

Initialization, scope, and uncertainty
This development uses genuinely saved step-0 shared within architecture; reconstructed initializations in historical multilayer diagnostics
remain grade C and must not be conflated with verified identity. Architectural parameter counts are 3,240,448 and 1,660,416,
with different initialization families/seeds, preventing separate identification of capacity and architecture. There is one new shared data world,
two relations, and one source per condition; 21 lengths are not 21 independent worlds, and 16 models are not 16 relations.
This round reports development results without added significance tests, confidence intervals, or confirmatory claims. Evaluation forwards include
task/equals suffixes, while measurement is before the task; causal models make this state independent of the current
suffix. Weights still arise from task training, so these should not be called wholly task-independent representations.

Review conclusion
Reportable: in this development unit, encoding changes initialization preference and the relative reshaping of correct/
wrong pairs during training; encoding GammaDelta direction is stable across architectures, prespecified metrics, and lengths.
However, trained Bt does not universally reverse, source-learning differences are large, and output geometry shows similar
interactions. These results do not establish an independent mathematical-encoding mechanism or cross-architecture confirmation.
Stop after this review; no additional training, formal manifest, or new domain was recommended or started.
