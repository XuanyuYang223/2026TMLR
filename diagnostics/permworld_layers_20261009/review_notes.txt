Initialization and world identity
All 164 extraction records remain grade C: reconstructed with the historical construction procedure; the original step-0 weight
identity is unverified. original_initial_identity_verified is false throughout, and source_updates
is 0 throughout. Parameter digests in the cache match inherited activation-archive metadata, but this only verifies
consistency of this reconstruction/loading, not the identity of the actual initialization used for training.

The 82 historical checkpoints are not 82 independent data worlds. This diagnostic shares one fixed evaluation-input
world, and each training cohort is interpreted according to its own historical provenance. Different properties/relations can
share source models and mathematical orbits; the 82 reconstructed initialization conditions have only 10 distinct parameter digests.
These 10 digests do not increase the number of independent training worlds either.

Display wording that must be corrected
The frozen summary template's statement “the between-source standard deviation reflects only initialization differences within the shared world” is overly precise.
Recommended formal wording:
  Between-source dispersion describes training-seed variability within fixed tasks and the shared evaluation world, potentially
  including initialization, dropout, batch/data order, and other optimization randomness. This design cannot
  precisely separate those sources or produce confidence intervals for independent new data worlds.
If the body uses “initialization differences,” it must include the qualification above; it cannot claim that only initialization changes.
This correction concerns presentation and interpretation only; it does not change frozen numerical metrics or analysis definitions.

Input baselines and interpretive limitations
The embedding at the final token and the unordered mean of numeric-token embeddings are structural constants within a length,
and must be marked undefined; floating-point summation noise must not be treated as a signal.
Positional one-hot features are equivalent to a flattened permutation matrix: complementation, reversal, and inversion are respectively column permutation, row
permutation, and transposition, all orthogonal coordinate operations. Invariance of the static one-hot Gram matrix under these transforms is
a construction property; it cannot distinguish correct/wrong mathematical relations, and its Bt=0 cannot establish that encoding contributes nothing.
Genuine embedding input baselines and static one-hot baselines must be interpreted separately.

Within-length one-hot KNN uses exact Euclidean distances from integer Gram matrices and fixed sample-ID
tie-breaking, avoiding centered floating-point residuals that break numerous genuine ties. Hidden-state metrics retain the original implementation;
input/hidden KNN numerical differences alone do not establish a mathematical mechanism.

Reports should continue to separate B0, Bt, delta_correct, delta_wrong, and Delta. Positive Delta
can result from a decline in wrong-pair scores; negative Delta does not mean no learning. Answer stratification does not remove all
output, confidence, or prediction-error information; hidden Delta minus output Delta is not a mechanism decomposition.
This round is an exploratory diagnostic of fixed historical checkpoints, not a new confirmation experiment. CKA, neighborhood overlap,
and held-out Procrustes alone cannot identify algebraic mechanisms or causal function.
