Exact graph formulas and negative control

A_ij=1 means i->j; diagonal0. Outdegree d_out(i)=sum_j A_ij; indegree d_in(i)=sum_j A_ji. R(A)=A^T, so d_out^A(i)=d_in^R(A)(i). Maxima and minima over the same vertex set give both R relations exactly. C(A)_ij=1-A_ij for i!=j and0 otherwise, so d_out^C(A)(i)=n-1-d_out^A(i), and similarly for indegrees. Taking minima yields max_out(A)=n-1-min_out(C(A)) and max_in(A)=n-1-min_in(C(A)).

W is a fixed permutation of unordered vertex pairs, built by Random(20261010+n) shuffle and adjacent swaps; odd last pair stays fixed. Both oriented slots of a pair move together, preserving the low->high slot orientation. Thus W^2=Id; R^2=C^2=Id; W commutes with R because both orientations move together, W commutes with C because C complements every non-diagonal slot, and R commutes with C. They generate at most2^3=8 transformed arrays. W preserves edge count. WR/WC therefore match their respective R/C controls in n and edge count.
