II. Formula and precision checks

Original loss is full-sequence shifted cross_entropy(ignore_index=-100). Every sample has exactly two supervised tokens, answer and EOS. For N samples, original loss equals half the sum of the two N-term CE means. This identity holds in real arithmetic; different FP32-reduction orders need not agree bitwise.

The repair retains original random FP32 toy logits and converts values with toy.detach().double(), without resampling logits, changing labels/positions, or regenerating models. The same logits in float64 then compare:
  0.5×(answer-position CE + EOS-position CE)
with original shift/ignore-index loss, strictly requiring a difference <1e-12.

The FP32 difference also satisfies an explicit scale bound 8×eps(float32)×max(1,|actual loss|). This is a rounding tolerance for the current engineering fixture, not a universal numerical-error theorem for every input/backend. A high-precision formula check remains, avoiding concealment of formula errors by simply loosening FP32 thresholds.

actual.backward() still uses original FP32 loss; precision verification does not replace gradient computation. Original supervised-position gradient-mask checks and causal-forward comparisons remain. Training core.loss and BF16/FP32 settings are unchanged.
